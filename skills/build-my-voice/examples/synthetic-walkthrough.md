Everything here is invented for practice. None of the rules describe a real participant. Do not use these lines as evidence of the person running the skill.

## Candidate rule

`R01`: “State the situation in one short sentence, then ask for one concrete next step.” Scope: follow-ups and delays. Status: candidate, based on E01 and E03. Do not extrapolate a universal ban on longer sentences or a personality label.

Ask the participant to edit lines that do not sound like them. Do not add these to approved examples merely because the assistant wrote them.

## Direct correction

Before: “Unfortunately, unforeseen circumstances mean we must regrettably postpone our review. Would Friday suit you?”

Participant's fictional correction: “We need one more day to finish checking. Can we review it on Friday?”

Lesson proposed: prefer the concrete reason and a direct request for a delay. Ask whether this applies only to delays or more broadly. Record it as correction C01. If the participant confirms the narrower rule and approves the revised message, update R01 within that scope and add the redacted approved example.

## Fresh fourth request

Fictional brief: the workshop recording is ready, and the participant wants the team to add feedback by Wednesday. Do not invent a recording link.

Candidate draft: “The workshop recording is ready. Can you add your feedback by Wednesday? [Recording link needed]”

Return the missing-link note separately in a real draft if the participant prefers. Check that the request is factual, the voice rule is followed and nothing was sent. The participant still decides whether it sounds like them.
