## Human judgement practice

1. Open S1 and verify the company, publication date and planned event. Explain what S1 does **not** prove.
2. Reject T2 aloud and say which evidence would be needed to reconsider it.
3. Read the first opener aloud. Replace one phrase you would not personally use, then recount the entire body. Preserve the conditional pain and single ask.
4. Explain why Priya is not yet a proven champion and why Ellen is not automatically this purchase's economic buyer.
5. Confirm whether contacting the three people fits the account team's existing plan. Drafting all three does not authorise sending them.
