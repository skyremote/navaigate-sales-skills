## Practise the judgement step

Give a participant the raw excerpt first. Ask them to find one obligation that a plausible AI draft could invent. They should reject “Saira will complete the review by Friday”, identify the superseded demo, and explain why “Boardroom West” cannot be assigned to Mateo without evidence. Then ask them to rewrite the follow-up so it matches what the customer actually remembers agreeing to.

If the participant later says “the room speaker was our colleague Alex, and Friday was only a possible time”, version the workup and record that as **participant clarification**. Move the conditional offer to the seller side with Alex as the clarified owner; leave the finance discussion unconfirmed. Do not rewrite the original room label or fabricate a transcript quote. Carry the correction into the email, CRM notes and relevant hand-offs.
