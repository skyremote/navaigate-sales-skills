# Meeting brief template

Use these fields as a working structure, omitting empty sections. Keep the main brief to roughly one page; the source ledger can follow it. Replace the field descriptions with evidence, not guessed values.

**Meeting:** exact title, account, local date, start/end and time zone.
**Prepared as of:** date/time and time zone.
**Coverage:** sources actually reviewed and material sources unavailable.

## Outcome to leave with

One sentence stating the customer outcome and the decision or agreement needed in this meeting. Mark a suggested outcome as proposed unless the customer has agreed it.

## People and context

| Invited person | Evidenced role or interest | What matters for this meeting |
|---|---|---|
| Name | Source-backed role, or unknown | Relevant commitment, question or decision |

## What we know

Three to five useful facts, each with a source reference. Include the most recent customer objective, change, constraint and prior decision. Separate any interpretation into a labelled sentence.

## Commitments and decisions

| Commitment or decision | Owner | Due date | Evidence/status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Agreed action, or proposed action clearly labelled | Named owner or unassigned | Agreed date or not agreed | Completed with proof / open / completion unverified |

## Questions and suggested agenda

Use a short timed agenda fitting the event duration. For each part, give the question that will advance the meeting. Flag conflicts to clarify before relying on them. End with a proposed next step and how its owner and date will be agreed.

## Source ledger

| Ref | Source and date | Link or precise uploaded-file locator | Supports / limitations |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | Title and date | Real link, or filename + page/message | Specific fact; any missing context |

If no direct link is available, use a filename/page or message subject/date. Never manufacture a URL.
