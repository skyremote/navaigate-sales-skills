# Practice: correct an optimistic review

All companies, people and records below are fictional. Use only these inputs; no connector is needed. As of 21 October 2026.

## Evidence

- **S1, synthetic CRM export, 20 October:** Northstar Design pilot renewal. Internal target: 30 November. Rina, design lead, labelled Champion. Finance contact blank. Stage labelled Committed with no qualification notes. These are synthetic labels, not live record IDs.
- **S2, call excerpt, 19 October:** Rina: “The pilot is useful. I can ask Finance who needs to review the renewal, but I cannot approve it.” Rina also says: “We would like to reduce the weekly hand-off work, but we have not measured the hours.”
- **S3, customer email, 20 October:** Rina: “Our IT review is still open. Please don't treat next month's date as agreed.”
- **S4, internal note, 20 October:** Seller: “Rina replies quickly. I think legal will follow the last process.” No legal owner or current lead time is supplied.

## Run the skill

Ask for the map, all eight MEDDPICC rows, next three moves and loss case. Useful findings include:

- Rina is the only evidenced contact. She is a Supporter or an untested champion candidate, not a proven economic buyer. Relationship strength is provisional because repeated responsiveness is internally reported rather than demonstrated by a full contact history.
- Finance/approver, IT review owner and legal/procurement owner are coverage gaps. Do not invent names or mark unknown people as blockers.
- Metrics are Amber: desired outcome but no measured baseline. Economic Buyer is Red/unknown. Criteria and process are incomplete. Paper Process is Red/unknown. Champion is Amber/unproven. Competition is unknown, not an invented named competitor. A customer's explicit warning challenges the internal close target.
- November's target is within 60 days of the review date, so the workshop paper-process flag applies. It is not a buyer-agreed deadline.
- A first sensible move is to establish the approval/review path, followed by an IT review check and a customer-owned outcome baseline. Owners and dates are proposals.

## The judgement exercise

Inspect three consequential ratings or lines. Explain why you would change or retain them. Do not change a rating merely to satisfy a quota. Record: “The draft assessed ___ as ___; I changed/retained it because ___.”

Now supply this fictional correction:

> Seller update S5, 21 October: “Rina arranged a call with Finance owner Ellis for Friday. This is my report, not an attached customer acceptance. Ellis's approval limit is still unknown.”

Rina has now reportedly taken an action; her champion evidence improves, with the internal-report limitation preserved. Ellis is named, with budget authority and attendance unconfirmed. Do not turn Ellis Green or call the meeting accepted. Rerank the moves: verifying that introduction and preparing the approval questions may now come before requesting an introduction. Preserve the older assessment and correction reason.

Finish by naming the one action to take this week. No message, calendar entry, forecast or CRM record is changed by the exercise.
