# Fictional worked example: adoption rose, value still needs measuring

All organisations, people, records and figures below are synthetic. No live data was retrieved.

## Supplied evidence

- **S1 — `customer-objectives.md`, agreed 2 July 2026:** Harbour Design wants to reduce weekly reporting rework. No baseline or savings target was recorded. The next QBR is for the operations team.
- **S2 — `usage.csv`, exported 3 October:** Q3 2026: 30 active of 50 eligible users. Q2: 18 active of 40 eligible users. Both use the definition “at least one completed report during the quarter”; eligible means assigned access throughout the final month. Reporting time zone: Europe/London.
- **S3 — `crm-export.csv`, exported 5 October:** “Adoption 80%”, no denominator, calculation period or definition.
- **S4 — `support.csv`, Q3 cases:** Five cases were raised; four closed; one remains open. Closure reasons do not establish whether the reporting rework objective improved.
- **S5 — `previous-review.md`, 2 July:** Our team committed to a reporting workshop by 31 August. `workshop-record.md`, dated 25 August, records that it happened. No before/after task timings were captured.

## Resulting executive brief

**Harbour Design — Q3, 1 July–30 September 2026; comparison Q2. As of 5 October 2026. Audience: operations team.**

**Customer objective:** Reduce weekly reporting rework [S1]. The outcome is clear, but an agreed baseline and target are missing.

**Progress:** Thirty users completed at least one report in Q3, compared with 18 in Q2. The reported active-user rate rose from 45% to 60%, or 15 percentage points [S2]. The eligible population also increased from 40 to 50, so this is a comparison of quarterly populations, not proof that the same users improved. It shows increased use under the supplied definition; it does not establish reduced rework.

**Delivery:** The reporting workshop took place on 25 August, ahead of the agreed 31 August date [S5]. Four of five support cases closed; one remains open [S4]. The impact of that open case on reporting is not documented.

**Evidence to resolve:** CRM says adoption is 80%, but gives no definition, denominator or period [S3]. Use the explicit usage-export measure for this draft and show the conflict. Do not treat the CRM figure as disproved until its scope is understood.

**Business value:** Time savings and ROI are not yet measured. Neither the workshop nor the usage count proves that rework fell.

**Decisions needed:** Agree a reporting-rework measure and owner, clarify the CRM adoption figure, and confirm the open support case's effect on the reporting workflow.
