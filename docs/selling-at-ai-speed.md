# Selling at AI Speed — four skills you can use today

This is the practical conversion of NavAIgate's original four workshop agents. The outputs and participant judgement exercises are preserved, with clearer source coverage, draft status and tool boundaries. No coding is required.

## Get started

- **Cowork:** download the [four-skill plugin](https://github.com/skyremote/navaigate-sales-skills/releases/latest/download/selling-at-ai-speed-plugin-v1.1.0.zip), open Cowork's **Customize > Plugins** and use the custom plugin upload control. Its name is `selling-at-ai-speed`.
- **Claude Code:** follow the [marketplace installation](platforms.md). The full `navaigate-sales` plugin includes these four and the five supporting skills. Existing users should update the plugin and verify version 1.1.0.
- **ChatGPT online:** download the [four self-contained Markdown files](https://github.com/skyremote/navaigate-sales-skills/releases/latest/download/selling-at-ai-speed-chatgpt-v1.1.0.zip), upload the relevant file to a private Project and instruct ChatGPT to follow it. Each includes its templates and examples. Connect approved apps separately, or use approved attachments.

Start with one skill. Use its fictional practice example before an approved real account. You do not need Outlook or Salesforce access to complete the practice. Check [platform setup](platforms.md) for details; a downloaded file does not grant account access.

## The four tasks

| Skill | Give it | Inspect | Correct before using |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Deal Health Reviewer](../skills/deal-health-reviewer/SKILL.md) | Account export, calls, email context and current plan | Committee map, MEDDPICC evidence, next three moves, loss case | Relationship labels, buying authority, evidence and action ranking |
| [Whitespace Mapper](../skills/whitespace-mapper/SKILL.md) | Buying centres, product footprint, usage and customer needs | Matrix and up to three plays ranked by value, effort and readiness | Budget, timing, politics, rollout status and the chosen thesis |
| [Basho Engine](../skills/basho-engine/SKILL.md) | Exact account, recipient or role, verified research and a relevant play | Trigger/date/source, rejected claims and short multi-role drafts | Source validity, customer relevance, recipient and your own wording |
| [Post-Meeting Agent](../skills/post-meeting-agent/SKILL.md) | Transcript, meeting date/timezone and prior assessment if available | Recap, both sides' commitments, MEDDPICC comparison, follow-up and handoffs | Who actually agreed to what, dates, changed plans and email meaning |

## One account, one reviewed sequence

1. Run **Post-Meeting Agent** on the call. Correct the commitments and follow-up before relying on them. Keep proposals distinct from promises.
2. Copy its reviewed Deal Health handoff into **Deal Health Reviewer**. Inspect the map and qualification, record corrections and rerank the next moves. The Black Hat skill is also available for a dedicated challenge; it now includes this inspection foundation.
3. Give the relevant needs and account footprint to **Whitespace Mapper**. Missing data stays Unknown. Correct the ranking and choose one defensible play; an existing entitlement may call for adoption work rather than another sale.
4. Give the chosen play, recipient and permitted evidence to **Basho Engine**. Verify an event before claiming it, then edit the short drafts until you would send them yourself. They remain unsent.
5. If a meeting is actually agreed, prepare for it and repeat the process. A handoff does not book a meeting or run the next skill automatically.

Pass only the relevant context: account/decision, as-of date, source references, confirmed facts, open questions, corrections and review status. Do not copy a full mailbox across products. Use your approved private workspace for customer work.

## A short room exercise

Choose one fictional example from the selected skill's references. Run it, then discuss:

- Which source supports the most consequential claim?
- Which label or commitment needs correction, and why?
- What is still unknown?
- What changes in the next action after your correction?

For Deal Health, inspect three consequential ratings or relationship lines; explain what you change or retain. For Whitespace, select one play and explain which you park. For Basho, reject an unsupported trigger and rewrite the opener. For Post-Meeting, verify both sides' commitments and correct the email. Keep the reason for a correction, not just the final wording.

Finish by naming one account, one skill and the next useful action you will take. The skill prepares the work; you remain responsible for its meaning and any external action.

## What was converted

The conversion was grounded in the original **Selling at AI Speed** deck, **Sales AI Playbook**, all four exercise sheets, the agent knowledge base and relevant workshop rubrics. It is a skill implementation of those workflows, not a byte-for-byte export of the original custom GPT configurations, which were not located in the reviewed source set.

No original gated knowledge PDFs, access codes, actual account records or customer transcripts are included. The public references use original rewritten instructions and fictional practice. Workshop heuristics are identified as such; historical licensing, entitlement and commercial-process examples are not presented as current policy. Platform-specific setup is separate from the reusable method.
